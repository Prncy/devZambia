01

Laws & regulators

Know before you build. These are the statutes and agencies that shape what you can collect, store, and ship in Zambia.

DP

Data Protection Act, 2021

Mandatory registration with the Data Protection Commission if you control or process personal data. Appoint a DPO in high-risk cases and obtain consent.

DPC registration portal ↗
CS

Cyber Security & Cyber Crimes Act, 2021

Criminalizes cyberattacks and unauthorized access. Critical information infrastructure operators may need local data storage and registration.

ET

Electronic Communications & Transactions Act, 2021

Legal foundation for e-commerce and e-signatures. Online platforms must disclose supplier information, pricing, and terms clearly.

ZI

ICT Act, 2009 (ZICTA)

ZICTA regulates network services and device type approval. Most software apps don't need a licence, but check if your product touches infrastructure or hardware.

ZICTA official ↗
PS

National Payment System Act, 2026

If your app handles payments, you likely need a licence or authorization from the Bank of Zambia. Covers consumer protection and complaint procedures.

Bank of Zambia ↗
©

Copyright & Performance Rights Act, 1994

Code is protected as a literary work for 50 years after publication. Register your intellectual property with PACRA.

PACRA official ↗
CP

Competition & Consumer Protection Act, 2010

Prohibits misleading advertising and unfair trading practices. Be transparent about pricing, terms, and how you use data.

VT

Value Added Tax Act (ZRA)

Cross-border digital suppliers to Zambian consumers must register under the simplified VAT regime. Handle it through ZRA e-Services.

ZRA e-Services ↗
EM

Employment Code Act & Anti-Corruption Act

Distinguish employees from contractors to avoid tax and legal liabilities. Bribery and corruption are prohibited in both public and private sectors.

02

Deployment roadmap

From code to launch: the practical sequence for getting your app live in Zambia without legal surprises.

  1. Register your business (PACRA)

    Your legal identity for contracts, tax, and IP ownership. Register a company or business name online through PACRA or the ZamPortal / GSB platform.

    pacra.org.zm PACRA portal

  2. Register with the Data Protection Commission

    If you process personal data, which almost every app does, register before launch. Complete the self-assessment questionnaire through the DPC portal.

    DPC registration DPC official

  3. Set up tax registration (ZRA)

    Obtain a TPIN through ZRA e-Services. If you supply digital services, register for VAT under the simplified regime where applicable.

    ZRA e-Services ZRA official

  4. Choose your hosting infrastructure

    Local hosting reduces latency and simplifies data-protection expectations. International cloud gives you scalability and global reach, but make sure your data processing agreements comply with the DPA 2021.

    imbra (local) Hodi (local cloud) HostList Zambia

  5. Integrate payments (if applicable)

    Mobile money and card integration is available through Zambian gateways like Lipila, Bila, DPO, and LimePay. Check whether your model requires Bank of Zambia authorization.

    Lipila (Flutter) Bila API Bank of Zambia

  6. Secure your domain & SSL

    Register a .zm domain through a local provider like Zamnet. Most local hosts include free SSL certificates on shared and VPS plans.

    Zamnet

  7. Launch, monitor, iterate

    Deploy, set up error logging and uptime monitoring, and make sure your privacy policy and consumer terms are accessible. If you collect data from children under 13, implement parental consent.
